# Rewards

## Earning points

| What you did | Points |
|---|---|
| Each hour of focused Python | 10 |
| Finishing a day's lesson, including tests and answers | 10 |
| Finishing a day's bonus | 5 |
| Fixing an error without asking for help | 2 |
| Finishing a weekly project | 50 |

## Spending points

Replace these with things you actually want.

| Cost | Reward type | Your reward |
|---|---|---|
| 20 | Small | Snack or coffee |
| 50 | Medium | Guilt-free evening of gaming or a movie |
| 150 | Big | A good meal out |
| 500 | Huge | Something you've wanted to buy |

## Milestones

These are separate from points, and you get them for reaching the milestone itself.

- [ ] **Day 7:** first project finished
- [ ] **Day 14:** halfway there
- [ ] **Day 21:** you've built a tracker that saves its data
- [ ] **Day 30:** your big prize: ______________

## Rules

- Rewards come after the work, never in advance.
- Points are for real work, so time with your phone in your hand doesn't count.
- Missing a day costs nothing, and you never lose points.
